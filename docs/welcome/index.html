Bolus insulin calculator

Dosage Helper

Work out your meal and correction doses from the ratios in your care plan, and keep a log of where you meant to land.

Open Dosage Helper How to install

Free. No account, no tracking. It runs in your browser.

How it works

  1. 1

    Enter your glucose

    Type your reading, or nudge it with the − and + buttons, in mmol/L or mg/dL.

  2. 2

    Add your carbs

    Enter the carbs you are about to eat, take off any fibre, or pick a dish you have saved.

  3. 3

    Read your dose

    It shows the bolus that brings you closest to your target, rounded to your pen or pump, and the glucose that dose is meant to reach.

What else it does

  • A log of where you meant to land. Each entry keeps the target you aimed for beside your reading, so you can see whether your ratios are working, with a graph for each day.
  • Dishes. Save the meals you eat often and fill in their carbs and fibre in one tap, in portions or added together.
  • A recent bolus warning. If you logged a bolus in the last 4 hours, it reminds you that insulin may still be working.
  • Basal by time of day. Keep your morning, lunch, dinner and overnight basal doses and log them with each entry.
  • Light and dark. It follows your phone's setting, so it is easy on the eyes at night.

Your data stays on your phone

There is no account to make. Your routine, log and dishes are stored in the browser on your device and are never sent anywhere. Back them up to a file from Set your routine to move them to another device.

Install it

Installed, it opens full screen like an app and works without a connection.

iPhone and iPad
Open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
Android
Open it in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
Mac
In Safari, choose File, then Add to Dock. In Chrome, use the install button in the address bar.

A helper, alongside your care team

Dosage Helper applies the ratios you enter. It does not replace your diabetes care plan, and its suggestion does not count insulin that may still be working. Change your ratios with your care team, and take extra care when you are unwell, exercising, or a number looks surprising.
