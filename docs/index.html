About

Set your glucose and any carbohydrate you are about to eat. Choose mmol/L or mg/dL. Show fibre under carbohydrate when fibre should be taken off before the dose. Basal amounts are Morning (4am to 10am), Lunch (10am to 4pm), Dinner (4pm to 10pm), and Overnight (10pm to 4am). Changing a basal amount keeps the earlier amount for previous days. Save to log keeps the current time, glucose, carbohydrate, insulin, and an optional note. Check With basal to record the amount for that part of the day. It is not a separate number to type. In the log, Add an entry records a past reading, carbohydrate, insulin, and note for the day you are viewing, and you can remove an entry. With the default ratios, 10 g of carbohydrate raises glucose by 3 mmol/L (54 mg/dL), and 1 unit of insulin lowers it by 3 mmol/L (54 mg/dL).

This applies the ratios saved on this device. It does not replace your diabetes care plan. Confirm doses with your clinician, especially when you are ill, exercising, or the result looks unexpected.
